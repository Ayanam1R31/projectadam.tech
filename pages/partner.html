Partnership

Partner With Us

We have running hardware and full flight-performance analysis today. We're looking for research partners to help mature the ADAM powerplant from a working prototype into a characterized, flight-proven system through grants, industry-sponsored research, and joint development. There are three ways to work with us.

Research Grants & Programs

We're pursuing grant and program funding (SBIR/STTR, agency research programs, prize competitions) to mature reliability and demonstrate ADAM against a defined requirement. Affordable, high-speed, mass-producible propulsion is an active research priority, and ADAM is built for exactly that. If you're a program manager or technical point of contact, we'd welcome a short technical exchange.

UAV Manufacturers

We work with UAV and interceptor manufacturers on joint integration studies, providing ADAM as a drop-in R&D unit: a plug-and-play powerplant you can integrate and evaluate on your own airframe. Connect fuel, bus, and throttle, specify your voltage and amperage, and we build a complete propulsion and power system to your spec and share the test data. Built for teams that want VTOL plus jet-class cruise without developing a powerplant themselves.

Industry-Sponsored Research

Companies can sponsor a defined piece of ADAM development: a scoped research project with agreed objectives, milestones, and deliverables. Sponsors get direct access to the team, test data, and technical reports, and can receive a non-exclusive, royalty-free license to the ADAM and Lilith technology stack. In-kind support such as test facilities, hardware, and manufacturing time is just as valuable. Reach out and we'll scope a project together.
