Partnership

Partner With Us

We have running hardware and full flight-performance analysis today. We're raising the funding to mature the ADAM powerplant into a fielded system — through government programs, industry partnerships, and early-stage capital. There are three ways to work with us.

Government & Defense

We're pursuing non-dilutive funding and government contracts — SBIR/STTR, agency programs, and prize competitions — to mature reliability and demonstrate ADAM against a defined requirement. DoD is explicitly funding affordable, high-speed, mass-producible propulsion; ADAM is built for exactly that. If you're a program office or end-user, we'd welcome a short technical exchange.

UAV Manufacturers

We sell ADAM to UAV and interceptor manufacturers as a drop-in R&D unit — a plug-and-play powerplant you can integrate and evaluate on your own airframe. Connect fuel, bus, and throttle, specify your voltage and amperage, and we deliver a complete propulsion and power system to your spec. Ideal for teams who want VTOL plus jet-class cruise without building the powerplant themselves.

Early-Stage Investment

We're raising early-stage capital to accelerate development toward production. Funding partners can receive a non-exclusive, royalty-free license to the ADAM and Lilith technology stack, and we're open to other structures. Reach out and we'll walk you through the current stage and what involvement looks like.
