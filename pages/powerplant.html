ADAM Powerplant

A drop-in hybrid microturbine. Thrust, VTOL, and power in one package.

Overview

One unit. VTOL, jet speed, and power.

Wankel and turboprop hybrids give you onboard power but turn propellers and stay slow. Bare turbojets give you jet speed but no VTOL and no electrical generation. ADAM is one hybrid microturbine that gives you electric VTOL, jet-class cruise, and 6 kW of continuous power. No Rotax or Wankel hybrid can physically get there.

It's built to drop in. Connect your fuel line, bus, and throttle command, specify your voltage and amperage, and ADAM delivers a complete propulsion and power system to your spec, replacing battery power entirely. It runs on standard Jet A, kerosene, or diesel, so it fits the same fuel logistics as the rest of the force.
